# User guide

How to use Create, topic by topic. New to it? Start with the [README](../README.md)'s
"Hello, world!" and the programs under [examples/](../examples/). Coming from Processing? Read
[Coming from Processing](processing.md) first: it lists what works differently.

- [Programs](programs.md): how a program runs, `create` and `update`, `run`'s arguments and the
  CPU or GPU backend, what is a field and what a parameter, what lasts how long, the run's
  settings, file paths and scenes.
- [Coordinates, camera and autoscale](coordinates.md): where things are on screen, points and
  vectors, the camera, and the design size.
- [Drawing](drawing.md): shapes and your own drawable types, the background, transforms,
  antialiasing and clipping.
- [Style](style.md): colours, strokes, gradients, shadows, tint and blend modes, and text.
- [Input](input.md): the keyboard, mouse and gamepads, the cursor, and text entry.
- [Images and animation](images.md): loading and drawing images, sprite animation, and building
  an image pixel by pixel.
- [Geometry](geometry.md): the shapes and curves, and collision tests between them.
- [Randomness, easing and time](math.md): random numbers and noise, easing and tweens, and the
  clocks.
- [Audio](audio.md): loading and synthesizing sounds, playing them, and controlling each
  playback.
- [Pixels, saving and recording](pixels.md): reading back what was drawn, offscreen canvases,
  saving the frame as a PNG, and recording GIFs.
- [Data](data.md): CSV/TSV tables and JSON.
- [Coming from Processing](processing.md): what works differently, and where the calls you know
  are.
