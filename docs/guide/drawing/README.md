# Drawing

How to draw on the screen and how what you draw looks: shapes and transforms, the style and its
parts, text and reading back the pixels of what was drawn.

- [The canvas](canvas.md): shapes, the background, transforms, antialiasing and clipping.
- [Style](style.md): how the style works, and tint, opacity and blend modes.
- [Colours](colours.md): making and mixing colours.
- [Strokes](strokes.md): outlines, their ends and dashes.
- [Gradients](gradients.md): fills that blend between colours.
- [Shadows](shadows.md): drop shadows and inset shadows.
- [Text](text.md): fonts, alignment and wrapping.
- [Pixels](pixels.md): reading the screen, and building an image pixel by pixel.
